# Swolempic

**Ozempic Makes You Smaller. Swolempic Makes You a God.**

A parody pharmaceutical website that asks the question everyone's been too afraid to ask: *What if instead of losing weight, I got absolutely jacked?*

Swolempic is an anabolic-androgenic steroid for people who look at GLP-1 commercials and think "I want the opposite of that." Built for anyone tired of being told to eat less, ready to intimidate doorframes, and prepared to file a formal complaint against their old wardrobe.

## What Is This?

It's a single-page parody site that pretends to sell an anabolic steroid positioned as the "anti-Ozempic" — a miracle compound that makes you gain weight, break chairs, and develop Main Character Energy. Every feature is a joke. Zero of the medical claims are real. The FDA has not approved Swolempic. The FDA is intimidated by Swolempic. They're "reviewing the data" — translation: avoiding eye contact.

Please don't take medical advice from parody websites. Your liver is watching.

## Features

- **Sliding news ticker** — CNN-style crawl with headlines like "Novo Nordisk demands Swolempic recall — 'It's making our drugs look weak,' says CEO"
- **Fake quiz** — 3 questions, must answer "correctly" to proceed. Wrong answers get you shamed. Right answers get you huge.
- **Before/after slider** — draggable image comparison. Results not typical. Results are insane.
- **Comparison table** — Swolempic vs Ozempic / Wegovy / Mounjaro across 17 rows. Swolempic wins every single one. The table is not biased. The table is correct.
- **Wheel of Side Effects** — 8 segments including "Doorframe Navigation Difficulty" and "Bicep Vein Satellite Visibility." Spin responsibly. Side effects may include becoming the main character.
- **Dose Calculator** — Enter your weight, goal ("Get Huge" / "Become Unrecognizable" / "Exceed Human Limits"), and experience. Get your exact dosage in scoops. The gauge goes to 11.
- **FAQ Accordion** — 7 burning questions answered, including "Will Swolempic make my girlfriend leave me?" (yes, but you'll be too jacked to care) and "Will Swolempic make me aggressive?" (define aggressive)
- **Breaking News Generator** — 22 headlines with gems like "Man on Swolempic Mistaken for a Fridge; Owner Claims Him at Lost & Found"
- **Gyno Checker** — patented chest-scanning algorithm. Your nipples are either FLAT, SUSPICIOUS, or PLANNING A MUTINY.
- **The Pump Meter** — slider from "Not Even Close" to "You Can Hear Your Own Heartbeat." Be honest with yourself.
- **Hair Loss Tracker** — 3-stage tragedy: REASSURING → RECEDING → FIVEHEAD. Your hairline is not what it used to be. Neither are your biceps.
- **Liver Status Gauge** — random fill bar. Verdicts range from "Healthy" to "Your Liver Has Left a Complaint — HR has been notified."
- **Stack Configurator** — 27 combinations of base compound, add-on, and support. Warning: some combinations cause your endocrine system to file a restraining order.
- **Blast & Cruise mode** — toggle between aggressive red theme and chill teal cruise phase. localStorage remembers your choice. Your organs don't.
- **Natty or Not Quiz** — 4 BuzzFeed-style questions. The algorithm knows. The algorithm judges.
- **PCT Section** — post-cycle therapy: three cards of regret and an appointment button that goes nowhere.
- **Dark patterns** — exit-intent popup, countdown timer, fake viewer counter, fake social proof, fake map of fake clinics. Pure e-pharma dystopia.
- **Drug Facts section** — 200+ lines of surprisingly accurate anabolic steroid pharmacology, complete with WADA detection windows, metabolic pathways, and evidence tables. Presented with the straightest face possible while the rest of the site calls you small.
